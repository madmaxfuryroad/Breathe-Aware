Background Info

A moderate AQI means the air quality is generally okay, but some people, like those with lung diseases or allergies, might be a little bothered by it. Many countries have areas with moderate air quality, especially in cities or industrial zones. Places with lots of green spaces, like parks and forests, often have better air quality. Countries in Europe and North America tend to have better overall air quality, with many areas reporting moderate AQI. However, it's important to remember that air quality can change depending on the weather, time of year, and nearby activities.  

While moderate AQI is generally safe for most people, it's always a good idea to check the air quality before heading outside, especially if you have any health concerns.
