Background Info

Bad air quality means the air around you is unhealthy to breathe. It's like being stuck in a smoky room – it's hard to breathe and it makes you feel sick. When the AQI (Air Quality Index) is bad, there's a lot of pollution in the air. This can cause coughing, wheezing, and other health problems, especially for people with asthma or allergies. Imagine trying to run a race while someone is blowing smoke in your face – that's how it feels to breathe in bad air.

Places with bad air quality often have smog, which is a gray haze that covers the sky. You might see less sunlight and it can be hard to see far away. It's important to stay indoors and avoid strenuous activity when the air quality is bad. Wearing a mask can help protect you from breathing in harmful pollutants.
