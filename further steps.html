Individual Steps

Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

    Walk, bike, or take public transportation instead of riding in a car whenever possible
    Use less energy by turning off lights and electronics when not in use
    Reduce, Reuse, Recycle to cut down on waste
    Use renewable energy in order to cut out fossil fuel usage

Be Mindful of Your Surroundings

    Plant trees and other plants around your home, as they help clean the air.
    Properly displose of chemical and pollutatnts.
    Avoid burning leaves or other materials outdoors.

Community Steps

Transportation

    Support carpooling and ride-sharing This can reduce the number of cars on the road, hence lowering CO emmisions into the atmosphere.
    Promote biking and walking: creating safe bike lanes and pedestrian paths can encourage people to use these eco-friendly options.

Education and Awareness

    Educate the community about air pollution: teach people about the causes and effects of air pollution.
    Organize community clean-up events: Get everyone involved in keeping the community clean!

Energy

    Invest in renewable energy sources: using solar, wind, or hydro power can reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
    Improve energy efficiency in buildings: this can reduce energy consumption and lower pollution.
